# Spruce and oak drafts

Tall spruce (16x32), young spruce (16x16), and oak (16x16), generated individually with built-in image_gen using Tiny Town and Tiny Farm references. Exact prompts in prompts.json. Source images, native PNGs and 8x previews preserved. Exporter fits visible alpha bounds proportionally within a one-pixel margin, nearest-neighbor sampling.

Dimensions and transparent top-left corners verified. Layered spruce silhouettes and round oak crown are present, with brown trunks and ground shadows. Generated gradients and edge fringes violate the flat limited palette; the small spruce is narrower than the square canvas and oak trunk is relatively large. Final style and scale review remains open. No game integration.
