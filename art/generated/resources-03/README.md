# Remaining resources

Built-in image_gen generated Leather, Gold, Chain, Mechanism, Remedy, Research points, Reeds, Feathers, Flax, Grain, and Vigor drink from section 1 of docs/sprites.md. Full prompts are saved in prompts.json; both Kenney sheets were style references.

Each resource has an untouched source PNG, a 16×16 nearest-neighbor export with transparent margins, and an 8× enlarged preview. These are generated assets pending the complete collection's final style audit, not integrated replacements.

Known audit items: generator shading is richer than the reference palette, the chain is more diagonal than S-shaped, and small details in gears, grain, and scroll symbols may need simplification. Exact silhouettes and palette compliance must be checked before declaring the entire catalog finished.
