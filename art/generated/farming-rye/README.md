# Rye sprites — draft batch

Three growth stages and one harvested sheaf generated separately with built-in image_gen and the original Kenney sheets as style references. Exact prompts are in prompts.json. Sources, 16x16 nearest-neighbor exports and 8x previews are preserved.

All four exports passed dimension and transparent-corner checks. Native-size review: green and golden stages are distinct, but ripe heads/whiskers lose definition and show a strong red edge artifact. Sprout greens are too neon, thin stalks break during downsampling, and sheaf binding is weak. Palette, silhouette continuity, edge cleanup and planting-anchor alignment remain required. No game integration performed.
