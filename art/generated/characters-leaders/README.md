# Tsar and Elder — drafts

Generated separately with built-in image_gen using Tiny Town and Tiny Farm reference sheets. Full prompts in prompts.json. Each has its original source, a 16x16 nearest-neighbor export and 8x preview. Export fits visible bounds proportionally, centered horizontally with feet aligned to the bottom.

Dimensions and transparent corners pass. Native-size review fails final style/readability approval: the Tsar source is too detailed, the staff becomes fragmented and its gold trim, face and beard lose definition. The Elder beard and hat remain recognizable, but the chest cross becomes an indistinct patch and the crooked staff needs cleanup. Sources introduce gradients and edge artifacts instead of flat pixel colors.

Tsar export has 74 distinct opaque RGB colors and zero exact matches to the required four skin/hair colors; recoloring needs correction. Elder uses fixed colors but has 92 opaque RGB colors, above the intended limited palette. Both remain drafts. No game integration performed.
