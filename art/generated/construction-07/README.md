Four 48x48 drafts generated with built-in image_gen edit mode and the finished bakery reference. Full prompts are in prompts.json. Sources, full-canvas nearest-neighbor exports and 8x previews are preserved.

Stages: ground stakes/string, stone base, unfinished clay dome, half-thatched roof over cold oven. All previews inspected. Foundation thickness, footprint and oven proportions vary between stages; edge artifacts and palette complexity remain. Final style/alignment audit is open. No game integration.
