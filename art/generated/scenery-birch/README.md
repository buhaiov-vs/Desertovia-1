Full-canvas nearest-neighbor exports: earth 16x16 opaque, tall birch 16x32, young birch 16x16 with transparent top-left corners verified.

Earth v1 incorrectly included a stone fire ring. V2 removes that feature and is selected by prepare.ps1. Earth still contains excess texture; seam compatibility is unapproved and tiny details lose definition at native size. Tall birch has a narrower crown than requested and green edge artifacts. Young birch occupies a small area of its canvas; scale, trunk readability and shadow need review. Both have a grassy base in place of the requested subtle ground shadow. Style and palette audits remain open. No game integration.
